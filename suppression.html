Deleting your data — Montage

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This page explains how to delete the data held by Montage (tel.thibault.montage), a video editor published by an independent developer.

No request is needed. Montage has no account and no server. The developer holds none of your data and has nothing to delete on your behalf. Everything is on your phone, and you erase it yourself.

1.Delete a project

In My videos, press and hold a project and choose Delete. This removes the app’s copy of the video, the cuts, texts, music files added, captions, transcripts and synthesized voices of that project. The original video on your phone is not affected.

2.Remove the AI models

In Settings › Manage AI models, press Remove next to a model to delete its file. Google ML Kit’s translation language packs are removed when the app is uninstalled.

3.Uninstall the app

Uninstalling Montage removes all of its private storage at once: projects, videos copied into them, models, preferences.

4.Exported videos

Videos you exported are in your phone’s gallery (album “Montage”) and are not touched by the steps above. Delete them from the gallery like any other video, and remember any copies you shared elsewhere.

5.Contact

Questions: support.app.bv@gmail.com. See also the privacy policy.
