Privacy policy — Montage

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Montage is a video editor for Android, published by an independent developer. Contact: support.app.bv@gmail.com.

The short version. Montage works entirely on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no server run by the developer. Your videos, your projects and everything the AI produces stay in the app’s private storage on the device. The network is used for one thing only: downloading model files (public files on GitHub, and Google’s translation language packs). The developer never receives your videos, your words or anything about you.

1.Who is responsible, and what this policy covers

Montage is published by an independent developer, referred to here as the developer. This policy covers the Android application distributed on Google Play under the package name tel.thibault.montage, and nothing else.

2.What the app handles, and where it stays

The app asks for no e-mail address, no name, no contacts, no location and no advertising identifier. Videos are chosen through Android’s own picker, so the app only ever sees the files you hand it.

3.What the app never does

  • No account, no sign-in. The app is usable the moment it is installed.
  • No server of the developer. Nothing you edit, say or type is sent to the developer or to any service on the developer’s behalf.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting. No such SDK is embedded.
  • No automatic cloud backup of the app’s data (allowBackup=false).
  • No sale, no sharing, no transfer — there is nothing in the developer’s hands to transfer.

4.AI features: everything runs on the phone

The three AI features work offline, on your device, with open-source models: speech detection (Silero VAD, bundled in the app), speech recognition (Whisper by OpenAI, run with whisper.cpp), voice/music separation (Spleeter by Deezer, run with sherpa-onnx). Your audio is never uploaded for processing.

Two of them need files that are not in the app itself:

  • Model files (speech recognition, separation) are downloaded once, at your request, from the app’s public GitHub releases. GitHub sees the download like any web request (your IP address, the file name); no data of yours is sent.
  • Translation uses Google ML Kit’s on-device translation: the language packs (about 30 MB each) are downloaded by Google’s library from Google’s servers the first time you use a language. Translation itself then happens on the phone. Google’s ML Kit terms apply to that download.

The translated voice is produced by the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (for example Google or Samsung TTS), through Android’s standard interface. Whether that engine works offline or online depends on the engine and its own settings and privacy policy, not on Montage; the app selects no network voice on purpose and works fully with offline voices.

5.Legal basis

The app processes your videos on your device to perform what you ask of it — that is the service itself (performance of the contract with you, and the developer’s legitimate interest in providing a working app). Downloading a model or a language pack happens only when you press the button that requests it.

6.Permissions

Montage asks for no dangerous Android permission. Videos and music are chosen through the system picker; exports are saved to the gallery through Android’s media store; the network permission serves only the downloads described above.

7.Retention and deletion

Everything stays for as long as you keep it. Deleting a project in the app removes its copy of the video and everything produced for it; removing a model in Settings deletes the file; uninstalling the app removes all of its private storage. Exported videos live in your gallery and are deleted from there like any other video. See the deletion page.

8.Children

Montage is a general-audience tool with no account and no data collection; it does not knowingly process any personal data of children, and it collects nothing from anyone.

9.Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Because all your data is on your device and never reaches the developer, you exercise them directly in the app (and in your gallery). A request to the developer can only be answered “we hold nothing about you”. You may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (in France, the CNIL).

10.Changes to this policy

This policy may change if the app changes. The date at the top states the version in force; the app’s Settings screen always links to the current one.

11.Contact

support.app.bv@gmail.com. See also the terms of use and the data deletion page.
